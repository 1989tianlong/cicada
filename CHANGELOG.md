# Changelog

One section per release, newest first. `scripts/release.sh` needs the section, dated today;
the release workflow publishes it as the release notes.

## 2.3.0 — 2026-11-03

- New (experimental): **Speaker identification** tells voices apart on your Mac and labels the other side's lines as Speaker 1, Speaker 2 and so on. Turn it on in Settings › **General** › **Experimental** (off by default); it steps in once at least two voices have five lines each, while one-to-one calls and unsure lines stay **Them**, and you can turn it off from the Experimental tag's "Not accurate? Turn off" link in the live notes window. With a Ling key, Cicada fills in real names from how people address each other ("Thanks, George") after two voices are found, every 20 lines and when the meeting ends, using those names in the live notes, **Meetings**, Markdown and Word exports, and **To-dos** owners while unrecognised voices read Unidentified. Click a name above the transcript in **Meetings** (clicking in the live notes opens it there) to see the line it came from, edit or reject it, or merge two voices; it runs on the bundled WeSpeaker ResNet34-LM model (VoxCeleb, CC BY 4.0).
- New: Turning on **Interpreting** now switches your Mac's system microphone to BlackHole 2ch and restores it when you turn Interpreting off or quit Cicada, so calls in Chrome or any app using the default mic hear your translation with no setup. Cicada keeps listening to your real microphone, which you can pin in Settings › **Interpreting** › System Microphone, or you can turn automatic switching off in Settings. If a crash or AirPods changes the system mic while you speak, the menu bar panel offers a **Restore** or **Switch back** button.
- New: **Show when sharing screen** lets you include the live notes window in screen recordings for demos and tutorials. It is off by default and lives in Settings › **Display**, the menu bar panel, and the **Display** › **Visible When Sharing Screen** menu item. Because macOS uses the same setting for screen sharing, the live notes window shows a breathing record mark beside its status while visible to others, and clicking the mark turns it off.
- New: The home page adds two interactive scenes under the three feature cards, and the Chinese copy reads more naturally. The speaking scene shows a call where holding right ⌥ lights your tile, transcribes and translates your words, and speaks them through BlackHole 2ch, with a button to hear the English or Chinese audio. The notes scene lays out **Conclusions**, the **Fishbone** diagram, and the original lines on one sheet, highlighting each conclusion or branch alongside the line it came from as you hover or watch.
- Changed: **Names and terms** in the notes now shows up to 8 entries, putting misheard names first with how they sounded, plus a "Show all N" button for the rest. Ling keeps the list to 10 and skips well-known names like Apple or NASDAQ unless they were misheard, while still using all of them to help translation.
- Changed: In the **Hearing** menu, languages not yet on your Mac sit in one group under **Downloads its speech model when chosen**, below **Auto-detect** and your ready languages, instead of showing a download label on every row.
- Changed: The live notes window and Settings › **Diagnostics** now show a preview outline before anyone speaks. **Notes** displays **First notes about 30 s after someone speaks** above placeholders for the summary, topics, **Conclusions**, **To-dos**, **Names and terms**, and **Fishbone**, while **Transcript** shows when listening started, two sample bilingual rows, and a blinking caret at **Now** that ignores stray background noise. **Diagnostics** maps out the stages of a sentence, the colour-coded translation routes, a faded sample sentence, and Ling as **Not used yet**, keeping **Copy Diagnostics** disabled until data arrives.
- Changed: Settings › **Interpreting** is half as long, with the on/off switch beside the title and one status card at the top showing **They can hear you**, the last delay, and **Try it**. The card opens into a checklist with one-click fixes only when something needs attention, such as the system mic, a meeting app on another mic, or Input Monitoring for right ⌥. Below it sit two plain groups without extra helper text, numbered steps, or an Advanced section: **Speaking** for your talk key, target language, and voice, and **Microphone** for switching the system mic, picking Cicada's input mic, and routing translated audio.
- Changed: The menu bar panel's **Interpreting** section explains what will happen the first time you turn it on (switching the system mic to BlackHole 2ch, holding right ⌥ to talk, and putting everything back when turned off) with **Cancel** and **Turn On** buttons, and shows the same on hover after that. While on, it draws the talk key with **They hear** (such as English · Tencent Meeting) and a green dot, lights up orange with **Listening…**, **Translating…**, or **Playing…** as you speak, and puts any mic or permission fix right on the key. The ⋯ menu is replaced by a Settings link and a "Try a line first" prompt until you test it, and turning Interpreting off shows only the languages.
- Changed: The menu bar panel's display section uses three illustrated tiles for **Display** (**Live notes**, **Subtitle bar**, and **None**) with the ⌃⌥S shortcut beside the title and a single card below for the active mode's settings. **Live notes** offers **Open** and **Hide** (which hides the window without opening **Meetings**) plus **Show when sharing screen**, **Subtitle bar** aligns its two timing controls at the same width, and **None** notes that the transcript is still saved. The subtitle content row above is renamed Content so **Subtitles** has one meaning, and segmented controls across the panel, Settings, and **Meetings** (**Both**, **Original**, **Translation**) now share a grey track with a white thumb.
- Changed: **Meetings** is now a single white page with the listening switch in the title bar: a white pill reading **Cicada** · **Start listening** (⌘R) when idle, a breathing ink pill with sound bars, timer, and **End** while listening, or a countdown ring with **Keep Recording** when a meeting is ending. The sidebar shows only meeting titles (using **Untitled meeting** when untitled, with time and length on hover) and groups older days under **Earlier meetings**, while an empty library shows only the logo, **No meetings yet**, **Start listening**, and a sample button. Each meeting opens in a narrow column with a grey info line, title, **Notes** / **Transcript**, **Export…** (including **Show in Finder**), an optional side-by-side original and translation view (off by default, remembered) that scrolls with topic times, **Conclusions** with highlighted numbers and dates, summary, foldable numbered topics, **Now** pinned at the bottom while recording, orange clickable times, and per-speaker colours when **Speaker identification** is on.
- Changed: The taller installer window now explains macOS's first-open warning while the developer certificate is pending. It shows three miniature screens with the buttons ringed in order: **Done** (instead of Move to Trash), Privacy & Security in System Settings, and Open Anyway under Security.
- Changed: **Getting Started** now lets you try live subtitles before setting up anything else, naming all four steps (**Audio**, **Try it**, Connect Ling, **Interpreting**) under the progress bar. It asks only for system audio on step 1, plays a short two-person call voiced by Ming where subtitles stay clear of the speakers, and explains what works if you choose **Skip** on the Ling key step (subtitles, bilingual transcript, Mac voices, and generating notes later). The **Interpreting** step asks yes or no before showing the virtual mic checklist and asks for microphone access only then, finishing with a pointer to the menu bar icon before opening **Meetings**.
- Changed: The website now credits @wquguru below the download button and in the footer, with links to X and the public source and builds on GitHub. On the setup page, the first-open card explains that Cicada is not notarized by Apple yet and points to its Apache-2.0 license, SHA-256 release checksums, and @wquguru on X.
- Changed: The website home page now features the same two-person meeting as **Getting Started**, tracking the March 3 launch discussion with live subtitles, a **Fishbone** diagram with dashed minor causes, the decision, the to-do with its owner, and a one-line **Interpreting** card. Clicking Play plays the call with sound (in Chinese on the English page) and lights up each speaker in step with their subtitle.
- Fixed: The **Transcript** in the live notes window no longer flickers or turns white during long meetings.
- Fixed: With **Speaker identification** on, Ling no longer assigns a name from a line spoken by someone else, such as an advert break, and rechecks past guesses. Names count only when spoken by that voice or within two lines of it ("Okay, Amos, thank you"), and well-known people follow standard news spelling ("Amos Hochstein") in both the speaker list and the notes.
- Fixed: Sentences split by a pause of up to 1.5 s from the same speaker are now joined back into one line and retranslated together in the transcript and subtitles, instead of staying as two half-translated fragments ("…at this" → "在这个").
- Fixed: Transcript lines no longer start with stray punctuation or carry long runs of dots and repeated commas from hesitations (", , that", "in..........", ". , that is"). Leading marks are stripped, dot runs become a single "…", and repeated commas collapse to one.
- Fixed: Lines consisting only of punctuation from a breath or click (like ".") are now ignored instead of being saved to the transcript and sent to Ling.
- Fixed: Clicking the live notes window or the **Subtitle bar** now closes the menu bar panel as expected.
- Fixed: A meeting where nobody ever speaks now stops listening quietly after the **End Meeting** silence time (10 minutes by default) when no meeting app is in a call, returning the switch to **Start listening** unless **Keep going** is chosen.
- Fixed: System Settings › Sound and other macOS processes that read the microphone no longer appear in **Interpreting** as meeting apps missing the virtual mic.
- Fixed: In the English interface, the live notes header, the menu bar panel's status ("English (detecting…) → …") and interpreting languages, and "· Change" in Settings › **Interpreting** now wrap cleanly without getting cut off or orphaned.
- Fixed: While holding the talk key and speaking Chinese, the **Subtitle bar** now shows your words about a second behind you instead of waiting roughly ten seconds or until you let go.

## 2.2.0 — 2026-11-02

- New: Forgotten meetings now end by themselves and trim the silent tail from the recording, so they no longer run for hours or swallow your next video. When a meeting app (Zoom, Tencent Meeting, Feishu, Teams) hangs up, the live notes window counts down 30 s with **Keep Recording** and **End now**, or counts down 60 s after 10 minutes without recognized words when no call is active (ignoring background music), and the next call starts a fresh meeting. You can choose **Ask, then end**, **End**, or **Keep going** and set the silence timer between 5 and 30 minutes in Settings › **General** › **End Meeting**.
- New: Settings › **Interpreting** now shows a live list of open meeting apps and the microphone each one is using. If an app is not on BlackHole 2ch, Cicada flags it there and in the menu bar panel with a **How to fix** sheet that turns green (along with the last step of **Getting Started**) as soon as you switch the app's mic.
- Changed: The live notes window now puts its title on the same line as the window buttons so more of your notes fit on screen.
- Fixed: When no meeting is running, the live notes window now plainly says so instead of showing **Writing notes…** and an empty outline under **Not recording**.
- Fixed: In fast conversations, especially in Japanese, long sentences now finish at the next short pause every 5–9 s instead of staying as drafts for 15–20 s while the notes wait.
- Fixed: Live notes no longer split the opening minutes into too many tiny topics and lump the rest of the meeting into the final one. Each live topic now runs for at least 2 minutes, and completed notes average about one topic every 3 minutes.
- Fixed: The live notes window now scrolls to the topic being discussed on each update, unless you scrolled the window yourself in the last 20 s.
- Fixed: When Translate into is set to **Follow system** on a Mac with both English and Chinese installed, subtitles, notes, and speak-back now share the same target language (preferring Chinese when present, then the first system language, and switching subtitles to the next language only when the other side speaks the primary one). The **Follow system** label lists the languages in that order (简体中文 / English).

## 2.1.0 — 2026-11-01

- New: Live Subs is now **Cicada** (**知了** in Chinese), with a new logo, app ID (`com.wquguru.cicada`), an Apache-2.0 open-source license, and a website in Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean (`/en/`, `/ja/`, `/ko/`) featuring a language switcher, three-step setup guide, searchable changelog, and RSS feed. Because macOS sees Cicada as a new app, it will ask again for system audio, microphone, and Input Monitoring permissions and start with fresh settings and AI key while keeping all your past meetings; you can delete the old Live Subs from Applications. The About window and Help menu now link to the website, GitHub, and issue tracker.
- New: The app interface is now available in English, Japanese, Korean, and Simplified and Traditional Chinese, following your macOS language across menus, settings, the menu bar panel, windows, **Getting Started**, alerts, permissions, and exports while keeping notes in your chosen notes language. Wording across the Chinese interface is also clearer.
- New: Cicada checks GitHub Releases once a day and shows an orange dot on the menu bar icon when an update is ready, or you can check anytime from the app menu (**Check for Updates…**) and Settings › **General**. Updating verifies the download against `checksums.txt`, bundle ID, version, and signature, asks to end any active meeting, and replaces and reopens the app without losing your audio or microphone permissions (or opens the release page if the app is in a DMG or read-only folder).
- New: Turn on **Open at Login** in Settings › **General** to have Cicada start quietly in the menu bar when you log in, without opening any windows.
- New: Settings › AI Service takes your Ling (ant-ling.com) API key, and **Getting Started** adds a one-click "Paste and Check" step right after permissions that notes Ling's ¥10 monthly free credit (with **Continue** once verified, or a skip option for later). The key is stored in an account-only file in Application Support that stays out of backups and never triggers Keychain password prompts on update (migrating any older Keychain key once). Without a key, Cicada works as before.
- New: With a Ling key, Cicada generates live meeting notes every 45 s during a call and rewrites them across the full transcript when the meeting ends, capturing a summary, timestamped topics and key points, a **Fishbone** diagram for root-cause discussions, **Conclusions** and **To-dos** with supporting line counts, and **Names and terms** with misheard spellings. Clicking any time range or timestamp jumps to that line in the transcript, the status bar shows sentence count and notes progress ("Notes up to 03:52"), and completed meetings get an automatic title in your notes language ("Nov 1 Lumen launch and checkout conversion discussion") that you can edit or reset via **Restore Automatic Title** in the right-click menu. In **Meetings**, you can switch between **Notes** and **Transcript**, click **Generate Notes** for older meetings recorded without them, and export notes at the top of Markdown and Word files.
- New: A floating live notes window is now the default view while listening, combining live notes, the transcript, and a live caption in one resizable window (a compact card under 360 px, tabs in the middle, or side by side from 760 px) that stays hidden from screen sharing and remembers its position. You can switch between **Live notes**, **Subtitle bar**, and **None** in Settings › **Display**, the menu bar panel, or with ⌃⌥S. While a meeting runs, the window shows live sound bars before speech begins, a spinner with heard sentence count and a shimmering outline for the first draft, an **Updating** pill as topics grow, and an **End** button (also triggered by ⌘R or the panel switch) that asks **Keep Recording** or **End Meeting** before finishing and offering to view the record or start a new meeting.
- New: With a Ling key, finished sentences are translated by Ling using the meeting's glossary to keep names consistent, falling back to Google and then Apple on-device translation if needed while live partial speech stays on Google. When you hold the talk key, Ming's audio model recognizes and translates your speech in one step in about 2 s (getting names like 江充 right far more often than on-device recognition) and falls back to on-device recognition if slow or busy.
- New: Choose the voice the room hears for each language in Settings › **Interpreting** (under the voice chip for the room's language), with **Play** buttons to preview natural streamed Ming voices (Emma, George, Charlotte in English; 小雅, 小棠, 小锋, 卓然 in Chinese; plus Japanese, Korean, German, French, and Spanish) or built-in Mac voices when using Cicada without a key. You can also record 15 s of your own voice to speak in any language with your own timbre (arriving about 1.5 s later than built-in voices, with your sample kept on your Mac and sent to Ling only when you speak).
- New: **Try it** in Settings › **Interpreting** tests speak-back end to end by sending your spoken or sample sentence into the virtual microphone and recording the other side. You can hear what the meeting app receives, see the timing for each stage (about 3 s with Ming voices, under 1 s with Mac voices, or 5 s with your own voice), and get automatic checks on virtual mic audio, re-transcribed accuracy, and (with a Ling key) preserved meaning, plus how to fix the first broken step if sound went to your speakers instead.
- New: Settings › **Language** brings every language option into one place under a live summary bar ("**They say** English → **Subtitles** 简体中文 · **Notes** 简体中文 / You say 中文 → **They hear** English"), covering what they speak, what subtitles show (**Original** + **Translation**, **Translation**, or **Original**, which does no translation and uses no Ling quota; also in the menu bar panel), what to translate into, the notes language, and the speak-back pair. For one-off changes, click the "EN → **ZH**" chip in the live notes window to switch subtitle and notes languages for the current meeting only, or click "Rewrite in English" on a finished meeting to regenerate its notes in another language.
- New: A **Diagnostics** switch at the bottom left of Settings adds a **Diagnostics** tab that records sentence translation times, which engine answered (Ling, Google, or on-device, plus Ming vs on-device for speak-back) and why any was skipped, Ling request rates and token usage, and a **Copy Diagnostics** button. Metrics are saved to `metrics.jsonl` in the meeting folder as timings and counts only, never what was said.
- New: While any Cicada window is open, the app appears in the Dock, ⌘Tab, and the top-left menu bar with menus for **Notes** (stop recording ⌘R, update notes ⌘U, **New Since Collapsed** ⌘J, copy notes, export ⌘E, **Meetings** ⌘0), **Display** (mode, card ⌘1 / sidebar ⌘2 / side by side ⌘3, stay on top), **Edit** (copy, paste including ⌘V for keys, transcript search ⌘F), **Window**, and Help, returning to the menu bar only when all windows close. Settings now opens in its own sidebar window (**Display**, **Interpreting**, **Language**, **General**, AI Service) where you can also customize the talk and subtitle bar shortcuts by clicking and pressing new keys (rejecting conflicting or modifier-free shortcuts with a clear reason).
- New: A sound test for the virtual microphone plays a sentence into it, listens back and shows what the room will hear.
- Changed: Hold the right ⌥ key by itself to talk instead of ⌥ Space, so you never trigger app launchers or type spaces; pressing any other key while holding it cancels speak-back so ⌥ + letter shortcuts still work. Cicada will prompt for Input Monitoring permission to enable it.
- Changed: Settings › **Interpreting** puts the virtual microphone checklist, talk key, and voice picker into a single three-step card, shows how long your last sentence took to reach the room at the top, and moves audio output device selection under Advanced.
- Changed: **Meetings** now reads like a document, grouping sessions by **Today**, **Yesterday**, **This Week**, and month above a live listening status card, and opening each meeting as a page with date, large editable title, duration, sentence count, languages, summary, and timestamped topics, **Conclusions**, and **To-dos** (plus ⌘F search). Opening Cicada from Finder, Launchpad, or Spotlight, closing the live notes window, or finishing **Getting Started** now brings up **Meetings**, while the live notes window waits until someone actually speaks before popping up automatically.
- Changed: The **Transcript** tab stays pinned to the newest line and shows the sentence currently being spoken at the bottom (keeping the dark caption bar on the **Notes** tab only); scrolling up pauses auto-scroll and collects new lines in a "↓ N new lines" button. Transcript search (⌘F) now highlights matches in place with a "2 / 7" counter and up/down arrows instead of hiding other lines, and matches misheard name spellings listed in the notes.
- Changed: When the live notes window is collapsed into a small card, it shows a "2 new topics, 5 key points since collapsed" button only when new items have arrived, opening a timestamped list on click. The **New** badge on points now appears only for a minute after a live update, rather than marking almost everything when a meeting ends.
- Fixed: When the heard language is set to **Auto-detect**, the opening sentences spoken in the first 2–5 s before the language is confirmed are now saved to the transcript and notes with their timestamps and translations.
- Fixed: Meetings are now recorded in a crash-safe format and converted to `system.m4a` and `mic.m4a` when the meeting ends (or on next launch after a crash or force-quit), so audio files are never left unreadable.
- Fixed: Changing the notes language while notes are updating now immediately rewrites them in the new language and discards the old-language reply. Full end-of-meeting notes also stay in your chosen notes language rather than slipping into the meeting's spoken language.
- Fixed: When a point is mentioned again later in a meeting, the notes now keep the timestamp from when it was first said.
- Fixed: Entering an invalid Ling key now shows an error message instead of silently asking for a key again.
- Fixed: Re-recording the shortcut that is already set, or clicking **Reset** when already using the default, no longer fails with a "taken by another app" error.
- Fixed: In the English interface, the language route bar, **Follow system** badge, menu bar panel interpreting line, live notes status line, **New Since Collapsed** button, and own-voice card no longer get cut off.
- Fixed: Installing the virtual microphone no longer looks like it failed. macOS only loads a new audio driver after its audio service restarts, so the same password prompt now restarts it, and if the driver is there but not loaded, Cicada offers **Load it** instead of reinstalling.
- Fixed: The menu bar panel now stays anchored below the menu bar icon instead of growing upward off the top of the screen.
- Fixed: **Diagnostics** now stops counting meeting minutes once the meeting ends, and **Getting Started** no longer re-prompts for system audio permission when listening is paused.
- Fixed: The live notes window no longer steals focus from your meeting app, brings other Cicada windows forward, or requires two clicks on its buttons while another app is active, and having only the live notes window open no longer puts Cicada in the Dock. Clicking the Dock icon reopens whichever window is open, export dialogs attach to **Meetings**, quitting during a recording asks for confirmation, and Settings remembers its position and last tab.

## 2.0.0 — 2026-11-01

- New: Cicada automatically detects the spoken language across your installed on-device models and translates into your system language or a language you choose.
- New: Manage everything from the menu bar panel, browse **Meetings** with Markdown, Word, and SRT export, and get set up with a three-step **Getting Started** guide featuring a built-in sample video.
- New: Hold ⌥ Space to speak your reply; Cicada translates your words and speaks them into the call through a virtual microphone installed right from the app.
- New: The **Subtitle bar** appears only when someone speaks, tucks into a small dot when quiet, turns see-through under your pointer, and can be shown or hidden anytime with ⌃⌥S.
- Improved: Speech recognition runs on-device with macOS 26 SpeechAnalyzer so no audio leaves your Mac, paired with a new app icon and DMG installer.
- Improved: The **Subtitle bar** now sizes its height to fit the translation.
- Fixed: Switching spoken languages no longer drops the beginning of the next sentence.
- Fixed: If translation fails, Cicada no longer speaks your untranslated words into the meeting.
