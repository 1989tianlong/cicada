建议README.en.md · Setting up an AI service key

Setting up an AI service key

OpenRouter (recommended) carries the same Ling text model and natural Gemini voices.

  1. Create a key on OpenRouter.
  2. In Cicada, open Settings › AI Service, pick OpenRouter at the top right, paste the key, and click Save and check.

Ling (Ant Group’s model service) has every feature, including your own voice, and gives ¥10 of credit every month.

  1. Sign up on the Ling open platform and click 创建令牌 (create token).
  2. Paste the sk- key in Settings › AI Service and click Save and check.

ZenMux, OpenAI or any OpenAI-compatible address work the same way. One-step voice translation and voice cloning are Ling only.

建议README.md（中文）· 接上 Ling 一节

接上 Ling API key

  1. 在 Ling 开放平台注册或登录，点创建令牌，复制 sk- 开头的 key。
  2. 在知了的设置 › AI 服务粘贴，点保存并检查。

注册不了 Ling？在同一页右上角换成 OpenRouter、ZenMux、OpenAI 或任何 OpenAI 兼容服务，纪要、翻译和自然人声都能用，少了一步语音翻译和用你自己的声音。Windows、Linux、Android 版暂时只能用 Ling。
